ppt-remote: present with your phone as a laser pointer, or with hand gestures

ppt-remote is a free presentation remote that runs in your browser. Open a PDF or PowerPoint deck, then change slides, point and draw with hand gestures in front of your webcam, or scan a QR code to turn your phone into a clicker and gyroscope laser pointer. No app, no clicker, no install.

Features

  • Phone remote: pair with a QR code; Next, Prev, Pointer, Undo, Clear and Blank buttons with a live slide counter.
  • Gyro laser pointer: hold Pointer and aim the phone at the screen; drag down to draw. Visible in the room and on video calls.
  • Hand gestures: six customisable gestures (next, previous, point, draw, undo, show text) tracked with MediaPipe in the browser.
  • PDF and PowerPoint: PDFs render locally with pdf.js and are never uploaded; PPT, PPTX and ODP are converted with LibreOffice.
  • Low latency: a direct WebRTC link over Wi-Fi, with automatic fallback to a server relay.
  • Private: webcam video never leaves your laptop.

How to use your phone as a presentation remote

  1. Open ppt-remote.onrender.com on your laptop.
  2. Drop in a PDF or PowerPoint file.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone.
  4. Press Present, then use the phone to change slides and point.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my phone as a presentation clicker without an app?

Yes. The phone works as a clicker in its browser after you scan the QR code. Nothing is installed.

Can my phone work as a laser pointer?

Yes. Hold Pointer and aim the phone; its gyroscope moves a red dot on the slides that everyone, including people on a video call, can see.

How do I control slides with hand gestures?

Allow webcam access and raise your hand: a raised pinky goes forward, a thumb goes back, an index finger points and thumb plus index draws.

Does it work with PowerPoint?

Yes. PPT, PPTX and ODP files are converted to PDF; animations are flattened to static pages.
